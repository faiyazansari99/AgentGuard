# Legal & Google Play launch checklist

Code cannot guarantee compliance with every law or guarantee Google Play approval. Before launch, configure the actual company identity, privacy contact, support contact, data practices, age/content declarations, permissions, SDK disclosures, account deletion flow, terms/privacy URLs, and any required regional consumer/privacy notices.

Do not claim certifications (SOC 2, ISO 27001, GDPR compliance, etc.) until the applicable controls have been independently reviewed/audited.
