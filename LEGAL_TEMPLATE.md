# Legal document templates

The project intentionally does not invent jurisdiction-specific legal language. Before launch, obtain reviewed versions of:

- Privacy Policy
- Terms of Service
- Data Processing Addendum (DPA)
- Subprocessor List
- Acceptable Use Policy
- Security / Trust Center statement
- Cookie/consent notice where applicable
- Refund and billing terms
- Data retention/deletion policy
- Incident notification procedure

These documents must reflect the actual company, data flows, vendors, jurisdictions, retention periods, and product behavior.
